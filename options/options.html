Tracker Blocking

Advertising, analytics, social, and other trackers are stopped locally with declarative network rules — before they load.

Phishing Protection

Known phishing and malware hosts are blocked on the way in. Redirects you dodge are kept in a local threat log.

Local-First Privacy

Visit history, threat log, and trusted sites stay on this device. Glass does not upload your browsing data.

Per-Category Control

Turn Advertising, Analytics, Social, or Other blocking on or off per site — or set the defaults once in Settings.

Company X-Ray

See past the domain names. Glass maps every tracker back to the company running it — so you know exactly who's watching, not just what.

Malware Scanning

Local ClamAV and YARA scan every download on this device. High-risk files are flagged so you can delete them or keep going.
