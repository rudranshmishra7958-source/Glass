About Glass

What Glass is

A local-first privacy layer that combines tracker blocking, phishing defense, download scanning, and company transparency — on this device.

How it works

Click a layer to see what it does. Glass runs them together, not as separate apps.

Tracker blocking

Declarative network rules stop advertising, analytics, social, and other trackers before they load — locally, in the browser.

Why this is different

Most tools cover one slice. Glass combines the slices so you are not stitching together a blocker, an antivirus, and a privacy dashboard.

Typical ad blocker

Stops trackers and ads on the network. Leaves phishing hosts and downloaded files to someone else.

Typical antivirus

Scans files — often after they land, and often with a cloud lookup. Does not explain who is tracking you on the page.

Glass

Blocks trackers, stops known phishing hosts, scans downloads locally (ClamAV and YARA when installed), and maps companies behind the domains — without uploading your browsing data.
